Getting started

  1. 1 Drag the button above onto the Blender window. Blender will prompt you to add the Vizable repository — confirm.
  2. 2 In Preferences → Extensions, find Vizable and click Install.
  3. 3 Open the 3D Viewport and press N to open the sidebar. A Vizable tab will appear.
  4. 4 Future updates show up automatically in the Extensions panel — click Update when prompted.

What's in v0.1.0

📷 Camera list

All scene cameras in one place. Click to set active, rename, or delete — no digging through the outliner.

🔲 Aspect presets

16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 9:16, and more. Selecting a preset updates the render frame immediately.

🎯 Camera tracking

Click anywhere in the viewport to drop a track target. Toggle tracking on/off; removing it holds the camera's look direction.

🌀 Depth of field

Click to place your focal point exactly where you want it — including inside the product. Controls f-stop from the same panel.

💡 Light controls

All lights listed with one-click expand. Strength, colour, and type-appropriate softness in one place.

🌡️ Colour temperature

Drive lamp colour from a Kelvin value (1 000–10 000 K) or switch to raw colour — per light.

🧭 Spherical positioning

Position lights by elevation, azimuth, and distance around your subject rather than moving them by hand.

➕ Quick-add lights

Area, Point, Spot, and Sun — each added with sensible product-vis defaults and placed in a tidy collection.

Changelog

v0.1.0 — Cameras & Lights June 2026

Camera panel

  • Camera list — click any camera to set it active; rename and delete from the panel
  • Lens controls — focal length, sensor width, clip start/end in one place
  • Aspect presets — 16:9, 4:3, 3:2, 1:1, 9:16, 3:4; updates render resolution live
  • Depth of field — click to place a precise focal-point empty; f-stop control; clear removes the empty
  • Tracking — click-to-place track target; toggle on/off; removing bakes the look direction so the camera doesn't snap
  • Save view as camera — captures the current viewport angle

Lights panel

  • Light list — all scene lights with expand/collapse; rename and delete from the panel
  • Quick-add — Area, Point, Spot, Sun with product-vis defaults
  • Colour temperature — Kelvin slider (1 000–10 000 K) drives lamp colour; toggle to raw colour picker
  • Size/softness — type-aware: radius for Point/Spot, width/height for Area, angle for Sun
  • Spherical positioning — elevation, azimuth, distance around a nominated subject object
  • Tracking — same click-to-place mechanism as cameras

Cross-cutting

  • Removing tracking or DOF deletes the associated empty — no orphaned objects
  • Retargeting moves the existing empty rather than creating a new one
  • Cameras and lights auto-placed in dedicated Vizable collections
